How it works

From file to finished, in four steps.

STEP 01

Share your idea

Send an STL, OBJ, or 3MF — or a sketch, a photo, a reference. No model yet? Describe it, and we'll help shape it.

STEP 02

Quote & material

We recommend a material, colours, and layer height, then send a firm quote and timeline before anything prints.

STEP 03

Print & check

Dialed-in machines run the job in one colour or many. We clear supports and quality-check every piece before it moves on.

STEP 04

Ship or collect

Packed with care and shipped to your door, or ready for local pickup. Typical turnaround: a few days plus print time.

Materials & tolerances

The right medium for the piece.

Not every object wants the same plastic. Here is what we keep on the shelf, and where each one earns its place.

PLA display & models

Sharp detail, huge colour range, dimensionally stable — the natural choice for models and display pieces.

PETG functional

Tougher and heat-resistant — brackets, enclosures, and anything destined for real use.

ABS / ASA engineered

Impact-resistant and paintable, built for mechanical and outdoor-grade parts.

TPU flexible

Rubber-like and grippy — gaskets, bumpers, cases, and wearable pieces.

Specialty silk · matte · CF

Silk, matte, glow-in-the-dark, and carbon-fibre blends — for finishes and strength beyond the basics.
